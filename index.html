About Us

Why we exist

We believe the best way to learn the Salesforce ecosystem is by shipping real projects — not just collecting badges.

V

Our Vision

To build the largest peer-driven Salesforce talent pipeline on campus — where every member leaves job-ready, not just certification-ready, fluent in both classic admin/dev skills and the new generation of AI agents.
